# 🇺🇸 US Local Business Sales Outreach Playbook
## Complete Guide to Selling Web Design & Growth Retainers to US Small Businesses

---

## 1. Why the US Market is 10x More Lucrative than Emerging Markets

1. **High Purchasing Power & Habit of Paying:**
   - US small business owners view $500–$2,000 as a standard operating expense, not a capital investment.
   - Monthly software & maintenance retainers ($49–$149/mo) are an everyday norm (they already pay for Square, Toast, Mindbody, Quickbooks, etc.).
2. **High Customer Lifetime Value (LTV):**
   - For an auto detailer in Houston, one ceramic coating job is $1,200.
   - For a dentist in Charlotte, a new patient is worth $1,800/yr.
   - For a roofer in Atlanta, a single job is $9,500.
   - Showing them that **1 new customer pays for the entire website** makes the decision a no-brainer.
3. **Fast Decision Making:**
   - Most US local businesses are owner-operated. You don't need committee approvals. If the owner likes the preview on their phone, they can pay on Stripe within 15 minutes.

## 2. Ideal US Outreach Timetable by Timezone

The US spans 4 major time zones. Schedule your cold calls and cold emails during their local business hours:

| Time Zone | Key Cities | Best Cold Call Hours | Best Email Sending Window |
|-----------|------------|----------------------|---------------------------|
| **Eastern (EST)** | Miami, Atlanta, Boston, Charlotte, Orlando, Tampa | 9:30 AM – 11:30 AM & 2:00 PM – 4:00 PM EST | 6:30 AM – 8:30 AM EST |
| **Central (CST)** | Austin, Dallas, Houston, Chicago, Nashville | 9:30 AM – 11:30 AM & 2:00 PM – 4:00 PM CST | 6:30 AM – 8:30 AM CST |
| **Mountain (MST)**| Denver, Boulder, Phoenix, Scottsdale | 9:30 AM – 11:30 AM & 2:00 PM – 4:00 PM MST | 6:30 AM – 8:30 AM MST |
| **Pacific (PST)** | San Diego, Seattle, San Jose, Portland, Las Vegas | 9:30 AM – 11:30 AM & 2:00 PM – 4:00 PM PST | 6:30 AM – 8:30 AM PST |

> **Pro Tip:** Never call restaurants or bars during the lunch rush (11:30 AM – 1:30 PM local time). Call between 9:00 AM – 10:30 AM before doors open, or 2:30 PM – 4:00 PM between lunch and dinner.

## 3. The 3-Touch Cold Outreach Workflow

### Touch 1: The Personalized Cold Email (Day 1)
- Send from a professional domain (e.g., `you@yourdigitalagency.com`).
- Subject: *Quick question about [Business Name] (+ demo site)*
- Insert the exact demo link from `script.md`.
- Keep it under 120 words.

### Touch 2: Cold Call + Text (Day 2 or 3)
- Call the store phone.
- Ask for the owner or GM.
- Deliver the 60-second hook from `script.md`.
- If they say "send me an email", reply: *"I already emailed you yesterday with the live link, but let me shoot you a quick text with the link right now so you can tap it on your phone."*

### Touch 3: Value-Add Follow-up (Day 5)
- Subject: *Re: [Business Name] website preview*
- Body: *"Hey [Name], just following up on the demo link I sent. Did you get a chance to open it on your phone? Happy to adjust any colors, pricing, or service names if you want to test it live. Let me know!"*

---

## 4. Payment Collection & Client Onboarding

1. **Invoice via Stripe or Wave:**
   - Create a clean Stripe payment link for **50% deposit** ($248 for Starter, $498 for Growth).
   - Set up recurring autopay for the monthly hosting retainer ($49/mo or $79/mo).
2. **Domain Connection:**
   - Ask the client: *"Who is your domain registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Google Domains)?"*
   - Either ask for temporary delegated access or send them the 2 A-records and CNAME to point the domain.
3. **Turnaround:**
   - Deliver in 24–48 hours. When you under-promise and over-deliver, US clients become long-term referral sources!
