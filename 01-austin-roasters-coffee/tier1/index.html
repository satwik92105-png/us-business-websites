🔥 Roasting Philosophy

Crafted on East Cesar Chavez Since 2012

At Flat Track, we believe great coffee isn't pretentious—it's dialed-in. We source relationship-driven, direct-trade green coffee from smallholder farms in Oaxaca, Yirgacheffe, and Huila.

Every batch is roasted in-house in small drums, dialed into peak sweetness and body, and poured by baristas who genuinely care about your morning cup. Whether you're here for a quick double espresso or an afternoon pour-over, you're part of our East Austin community.
