Common Questions

FAQ

Is Flat Track dog-friendly?▼
Yes! Dogs are welcome on the outdoor patio. We keep a fresh water bowl out. Well-behaved pups are always welcome.
Do you offer a grind service?▼
Yes — we grind to order for drip, french press, pour-over, or espresso. Just ask your barista or specify in the customizer.
Is there parking?▼
Free street parking on E Cesar Chavez St and Chicon St. Bicycle racks in front. Bus stop one block west on Cesar Chavez.
Do you do catering or corporate orders?▼
Yes! We handle office coffee deliveries and event catering. Contact us through the form below with your event date and headcount.
Do you sell wholesale beans?▼
We do wholesale to Austin restaurants and cafes. Minimum order applies. Reach out via the contact form and our head roaster will respond within 24 hours.
Can I pre-order online and skip the line?▼
Yes — use the Pre-Order section above. Select your items, pick a pickup time, and your order will be ready when you walk in.
