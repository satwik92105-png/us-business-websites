The Seed-to-Cup Craft

Scroll down to explore how our beans journey from high-altitude volcanic soil to your morning brew.

🌱

1. Direct Trade Micro-Lot Harvest

We partner directly with smallholder farming families in Oaxaca, Huila, and Yirgacheffe at elevations above 1,700m. Cherry selection is 100% hand-harvested at peak brix ripeness.

🔥

2. Small-Batch Cast-Iron Drum Roasting

Roasted on East Cesar Chavez in calibrated micro-batches. Our roast profiles are dialed into caramelize natural sugars without masking delicate floral, citrus, and stone fruit acidity.

☕

3. Precision Barista Dial-In

Every morning at 6:00 AM, our lead baristas refractometer-test and taste every origin on bar, fine-tuning water temperature, grind distribution, and extraction yield to 20.5%.

🚀

4. Delivered Fresh to You (Doorstep or Counter)

Whether picked up at our cafe in 10 minutes or delivered to your doorstep bi-weekly via our club subscription, you receive peak-fresh beans at their optimal degassing window.
