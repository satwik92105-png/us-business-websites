02 / THE CONVICTION

Why Flat Track Roasters?

Coffee should be honest, bold, and unpretentious. We built Flat Track on vintage motorcycles, small-batch drum roasting craft, and neighborhood loyalty in East Austin.

✓ 88+ Cupping Scores: Micro-lots personally sourced at origin.
✓ Cast-Iron Drum: Maximum caramelization & full sweet body.
✓ Direct Sourcing: Paying premiums 240% above fair trade floor.

01 / HARVEST

Direct Trade Micro-Lots 🌱

We travel directly to smallholder farming families across Oaxaca, Antioquia, and Sidama. Full seed-to-cup traceability ensures ethical sustainability and pristine coffee cherry sweetness.

02 / ROAST

Drum Conduction Profiling 🔥

Every small batch is roasted in our custom cast-iron drum roaster in East Austin. Conduction heat transfers deep into the bean core, producing rich chocolate crema and fruit sweetness.

03 / EXTRACTION

La Marzocco Precision ☕

Our baristas calibrate grinder microns, dual-boiler temperatures, and custom remineralized reverse-osmosis water (140 ppm) every morning before the doors unlock at 6:30 AM.

04 / COMMUNITY

East Austin Neighborhood Culture 🤝

More than a coffee shop: a gathering ground for mechanics, runners, remote engineers, and neighbors sharing real conversations and dialed-in espresso.

05 / SEED TO CUP MASTERY

Flat Track Brings The Craft

Five uncompromising standards that differentiate our East Austin roastery from ordinary corporate cafes.

01 / DIRECT SOURCING

Direct-Trade Farm Partnerships 🌱

We work directly with generational coffee producers across Central and South America, paying well above fair-trade market minimums to support agricultural longevity.

✓ 100% Traceable to origin mill and elevation
02 / CAST-IRON ROASTING

Small-Batch Drum Roasting 🔥

Our cast-iron drum roaster provides optimal conductive heat transfer, gently developing complex sugars without scorching delicate floral notes.

✓ Roasted fresh 3x weekly in East Austin
03 / WATER SCIENCE

Remineralized Water Chemistry 💧

Water constitutes 98% of your cup. Our custom reverse osmosis system balances mineral hardness (140 ppm) for peak extraction clarity.

✓ Daily refractometer TDS calibration
04 / CUSTOM EXTRACTION

La Marzocco Strada Profiling ☕

Independent saturated group boilers deliver continuous thermal stability, allowing our baristas to dial variable pressure profiles per origin.

✓ 9-bar pre-infusion & tailored yields
05 / SUBSCRIPTIONS & PICKUP

Peak-Fresh Doorstep Delivery 📦

Shipped within 48 hours of roasting. Choose whole bean or tailored grind, weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly with free shipping and member discounts.

✓ Zero lock-in, pause or skip anytime
