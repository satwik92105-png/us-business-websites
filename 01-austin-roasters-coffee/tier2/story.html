The Soul of East Cesar Chavez

Built on a passion for vintage motorcycles, small-batch roasting craft, and authentic neighborhood hospitality.

🌱 Direct Trade Craft

From Micro-Lot Harvest to Dialed-In Cup

Flat Track started with a simple conviction: coffee should be unpretentious, honest, and roasted to highlight the natural fruit sweetness of the cherry.

We travel directly to origin to shake hands with growers across Oaxaca, Antioquia, and Sidama. By paying premiums well above fair-trade floor prices, we secure the finest 86+ specialty micro-lots while supporting agricultural sustainability.

Inside our East Cesar Chavez roastery, each lot is roasted in small batches on our cast-iron drum roaster, constantly cupped and adjusted for Austin's palate.
