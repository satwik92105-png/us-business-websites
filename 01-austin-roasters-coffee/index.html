The Flat Track Philosophy

Ethical Sourcing

Micro-Batch Roasting in East Austin

We source our green beans directly from farm cooperatives in Huila, Yirgacheffe, and Antigua, paying above Fair Trade premiums. Every pound is roasted right here on Cesar Chavez in small batches to preserve terroir and nuanced aromatics.
