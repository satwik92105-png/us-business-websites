Farm-to-Cup Provenance

The Flat Track Bean Journey

Trace how raw high-altitude cherries transform into your morning cup on East Cesar Chavez.

STAGE 01 // 1,950M MASL

Direct Origin Farm

Hand-picked red bourbon and heirloom cherries grown in Yirgacheffe and Huila volcanic soil by partner smallholder farmers.

STAGE 02 // LOGISTICS

GrainPro Transit

Hermetically sealed green coffee transported under strictly monitored humidity to Port of Houston and trucked straight to Austin.

STAGE 03 // ROASTERY

San Franciscan Drum

Small-batch convection roast with PID temperature control, targeting peak caramelization and delicate floral volatile acids.

STAGE 04 // EXTRACTION

Precision Pull

Ground on Mahlkönig EK43 burrs and pulled at 9 bars of pressure with Austin's treated mineral-balanced water.
