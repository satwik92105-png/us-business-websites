Rooted in Austin

Roasting Without Pretense Since Day One

Flat Track Coffee was founded on a simple premise: source ethically produced green coffee directly from smallholder farms, roast it with uncompromising precision, and serve it in a welcoming, community-first space where bikes, art, and neighborhood regulars collide.

We never mask poor beans behind artificial syrups. From our signature Dogspeed espresso blend to our 24-hour slow-dripped nitro cold brew, every pour is calibrated daily by our barista team.

Direct Trade Sourced

We pay premiums above Fair Trade floor prices directly to farm partners.

Weekly Small Batches

Roasted in small drum roasters right in our Cesar Chavez cafe.

Community Patio

Dog-friendly outdoor seating, motorcycle parking, and free gigabit Wi-Fi.

Zero Hidden Fees

All prices include transparent hospitality taxes and fair barista wages.
